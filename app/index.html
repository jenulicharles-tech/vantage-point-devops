Our Exclusive IT Solution Services

User Management

User management stores and maintains all information related to Keells staff, including personal details, SAP numbers, job roles, contact information and system access data, ensuring accurate and centralized employee records.

Keells Intranet

Keells Intranet System, updates staff SAP numbers and maintains other essential employee details, while also managing access to applications such as Wooqer and systems used for picking online orders.

Keells Helpdesk

Keells Helpdesk System tracks and manages helpdesk tickets, providing visibility into software and hardware issues reported by staff for timely resolution.

Vendor Handling System

Vendor Handling System is used to coordinate with vendors who troubleshoot hardware issues on-site at outlets, ensuring timely support and communication with the relevant teams.
